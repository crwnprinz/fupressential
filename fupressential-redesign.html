Fresher Survival Guide

🎓 Welcome to FUPRE!

First petroleum university in Africa. Follow these steps to get settled without stress.

01

Get Your Admission Letter

Visit utme.fupre.edu.ng and print your admission letter. Keep multiple copies — you'll need it everywhere.

💡 Laminate one copy. You'll thank yourself later.
02

Report to the Registry

Bring: WAEC/NECO result, admission letter, JAMB result, birth certificate and 2 passport photos.

💡 Go early — queues are brutal. Arrive by 7:30 AM.
03

Pay Your School Fees

Generate invoice from the student portal and pay through Remita only. Keep your receipt.

💡 Never pay anyone directly. Remita only.
04

Complete eClearance

Log into the online services portal, upload required documents. Must be approved before course registration.

05

Register Your Courses

Go to your department after clearance. Your HOD or level adviser will guide you on what to register.

💡 Don't exceed your credit unit limit.
06

Get Your Student ID

Visit the ICT centre or student affairs office after registration. Your ID is your identity on campus.

07

Find Accommodation

Apply for hostels early through the portal — spaces go fast. Many students live in Effurun town.

💡 Join your departmental WhatsApp group for accommodation tips.
08

Join Student Communities

Find your course rep and class group chat. These are your lifeline for test dates, handouts and tips.

💡 Your course rep is more important than you think.
09

Start Strong

Attend orientation, meet your lecturers, explore campus. Your first CGPA matters — start serious from day one.

💡 First impressions with lecturers matter too.

FAQ

Is FUPRE a good university?

Yes — first petroleum university in Africa and 6th in the world. Employers in oil and gas specifically seek FUPRE graduates.

What courses does FUPRE offer?

19 undergraduate programmes across 5 colleges — all science, engineering and petroleum. See the Courses page for the full list.

What is the FUPRE cut-off mark?

Minimum UTME cut-off is 170. Competitive courses like Petroleum Engineering require 200+.

Where is FUPRE located?

Ugbomro, Uvwie LGA, Delta State — along the Effurun-Port Harcourt road, before Agbarho Bridge.

Does FUPRE have accommodation?

Yes, on-campus hostels — but limited spaces. Apply early. Many students live in Effurun or Warri and commute.

How do I pay school fees?

All payments via Remita. Generate invoice from the student portal, get your RRR number and pay. Never pay anyone directly.

What is the student portal?

online-services.fupre.edu.ng — eClearance, eBursary, eRecords and course registration. You'll use this throughout your studies.

Does FUPRE have postgraduate programmes?

Yes — PGD, M.Sc/M.Eng and Ph.D in several departments. Minimum Second Class Lower for Masters.
